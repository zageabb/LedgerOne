Due-date precedence

1. Explicit due date — if entered on an invoice or bill, LedgerOne keeps it.

2. Customer / supplier override — used when the document due date is left blank.

3. Organisation default — used when there is no contact override.

Quotes and purchase orders use the same rules when they are converted to invoices or bills.
